[Read the assessment with a worked example](https://jrefstrupalvear.github.io/workflow-field-guide/automation-opportunity-assessment.html).

Make one copy per candidate workflow. Add estimates for a typical week and keep a note of where the numbers came from.

## Worked example

For 30 hypothetical invoice exceptions per week at 12 minutes each, baseline handling takes 360 staff minutes. Removing 75% of the work saves 270 minutes before review. After 120 minutes of new review, the net recovery is 150 staff minutes per week. The proposed pilot advances if it recovers at least 60 minutes without misrouting an invoice. Suspected supplier mismatches go to a human, and payment approvals stay with the existing approver.
