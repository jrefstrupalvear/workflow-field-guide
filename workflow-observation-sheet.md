## Check the rule with the person doing the work

- What would make you stop and ask someone before acting?
- Which cases were missing from this sample?
- What would a wrong action cost, and how would you detect it?

## Decide what to pilot

1. Write down the exact action a tool could take and the evidence it would need.
2. Sum the active minutes for cases that satisfy that rule. Identify which minutes would actually disappear.
3. Subtract any new human checks, maintenance and exception review for the same period. Keep the unit in staff minutes throughout.
4. Record the remaining manual queue, the person responsible for it, and the condition that would stop the pilot.
