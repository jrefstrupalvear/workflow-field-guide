How to read the result

Net weekly hours recovered = (weekly items × staff minutes per item × removable share − added weekly review minutes) ÷ 60. Use the share of staff time the pilot could remove, expressed as a decimal in this formula.

Estimate the share from active minutes on cases the rule can handle. For example, if those cases account for 12 of 36 minutes observed, start with an upper estimate of 33%. Reduce it for any original steps people would still perform. Enter new oversight time in the review field.

Negative net hours mean the pilot adds work. If both estimates are close, test a small batch and compare incorrect outcomes and exception handling before committing.
